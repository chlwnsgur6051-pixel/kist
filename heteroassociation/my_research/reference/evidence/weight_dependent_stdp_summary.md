# Weight-dependent STDP mathematical experiment

**Scope.** Feedforward 15×15, current digit 0–9→letter A–J patterns, bipolar representation, static `score=X_b@(W_raw−0.05)` and `score>0` → +1. No LIF or noisy cues. This is a **mathematical sequential update simulation**, not a run of the original spike-event STDP simulator. Original unbounded correlation is reported separately. K=1,2,3,5,7,10 use all 10,45,120,252,120,1 subsets respectively. Every result is association-weighted within K. Files in `csv/` and `npz/` preserve raw scores, margins, weights, subset and order identifiers.

## Timing and baseline verification

Original B2 `encode()` and teacher time 4.5 ms were called for all 2,250 synapses in the dataset: pre 2 ms gives LTP, pre 7 ms LTD, and every direction equals `x_b*y_b`. Details: `csv/timing_direction_validation.csv`. The original trainer uses `A±=0.02`, `tau_STDP=2 ms`, hence event magnitude `A=0.02 exp(-2.5/2)=0.005730095937`, 60 epochs, initial raw W=0.05, bounds [-1.5,1.5], and `np.random.seed(0)` reset for each output column. The mathematical simulator uses exactly the same per-epoch permutation sequence and teacher-event direction, while skipping membrane dynamics that do not enter the original updater's selected teacher event.

Pure correlation `X_b.T@Y_b` reproduced exact accuracy K=1: 100.00%, K=2: 100.00%, K=3: 64.44%, K=5: 37.78%, K=7: 25.83%, K=10: 10.00%. ## Staged parameter study

Selection criterion is the unweighted mean of exact accuracy percentages at K=3,5,7,10, so K=10 is not the sole driver. Positive-gain μ values selected from the prespecified sweep: [2.0, 3.0]. Equal A± learning-rate factors 0.5,1,2 were tested first at original bounds; then symmetric bounds ±0.75,±1.5,±3 at the selected rate. LTP/LTD ratio 0.8,1,1.2 was tested only if the selected equal-rate setting beat its **matched μ=0 additive** control; geometric mean of A+ and A− was held fixed. `csv/parameter_selection.csv` records every stage and `csv/parameter_sweep_results.csv` preserves all tested K values, including μ=0 controls. This is same-dataset exploratory selection, not a general optimum. Highest equal-K mean among tested parameter conditions: (37.023809523809526, ('weight_bound', 'symmetric_bound_1.5', 2.0)).

`csv/parameter_matched_comparison.csv` places every tested weight-dependent condition beside its μ=0 control at the **same** rate, bounds and A+/A− ratio. The ratio 0.8 setting changes the additive baseline itself; a K=10 success under that ratio is not evidence that weight dependence alone caused it.

## Direct answers and limitations

1. **Additive baseline:** μ=0 matches the bounded additive rule and archived B2 static exact counts where available. Pure unbounded correlation matches all supplied reference rates.
2. **Accuracy improvement:** See table above. Any μ improvement must be compared with μ=0 at the same rate, bounds, initial W and schedule; original correlation is a separate unbounded reference.
3. **Effective μ:** The sweep and staged comparisons identify observed values, without validation on held-out patterns.
4. **Saturation:** The table and `accuracy_results.csv` measure terminal-bound ratio and attempted clipped updates separately. Lower saturation alone does not establish better recall.
5. **Order:** The order table gives within-subset variability and fraction of runs whose W differs from ascending; a best order is not treated as an improvement claim.
6. **Association tradeoffs at K=10, μ=2:** improved exact digits [2], worsened exact digits []; subset-level tradeoffs at K<10 are in `association_results.csv`.
7. **K=10:** matched additive 1/10 versus μ=2 2/10 at original settings; this is one extra exact digit in the only K=10 subset, not evidence of a statistically reliable or broadly meaningful gain. Parameter-stage K=10 results are all listed in `parameter_sweep_results.csv`.
8. **Simulator feasibility:** This rule is synapse-local and retains timing-based direction, but the original `stdp_update` magnitude expression at `Ver관리/2. temporal/stdp_temporal_symmetric_teacher_only.py` would have to change. The simulator's natural spikes, teacher timing, and clipping could interact with that rule; this mathematical accuracy does not prove actual spike-event performance or biological plausibility. No original file was changed.
